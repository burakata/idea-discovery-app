# Idea context: pallet transport (first project)

<!--

## Who
Three business owners are considering starting a pallet transport business in
the UK. One of the three is British; expect both English and Turkish in this
room.

## Starting picture (to be confirmed in the first discussion)
Prepared pallets are transported from one warehouse or business to another.
This is Burak's best current picture, not something the owners have
confirmed.

## Early interpretations from a short conversation
Check these naturally; do not present them as settled.

- The driver is not expected to pack the goods.
- Loading and unloading may not be part of the driver's role.
- There may be extra charges for waiting time.
- Jobs may be booked by appointment.
- Customers may include suppliers who currently deliver with their own truck
  and staff.
- They may start with their own van.

## Not known yet
- Who asks for a job, and how.
- Who sets the price.
- How a driver gets a job.
- How payment works.

## Similar businesses shown on the idea page
Courier Exchange, Shiply, DigiHaul and PalletOnline, as examples only. The
owners have not said their idea is like any of them.

## Do not assume
- Do not assume the business is a marketplace or connects customers with
  drivers.
- Do not assume how pricing, payment or commission works.
- Do not assume who the drivers are.
