## Who
Three business owners are considering a business where people who cook at
home sell meals to their neighbours. Two prefer Turkish and one prefers
English; expect both languages in this room.

## Starting picture (to be confirmed in the first discussion)
People who cook at home sell meals to their neighbours. This is Burak's best
current picture, not something the owners have confirmed.

## Early interpretations from a short conversation
Check these naturally; do not present them as settled.

- Customers may mostly be people who live nearby.
- Each cook may decide what to make.
- Meals may be ordered a day ahead.
- Each cook may make only a few portions at a time.
- They may start with a few cooks they already know.

## Not known yet
- How a neighbour asks for a meal.
- Who delivers the meals.
- How payment is taken.
- Who sets the price of a meal.

## Similar businesses shown on the idea page
A neighbourhood noticeboard, a shared kitchen, a weekly meal plan and a local
food stall, as plain descriptions of how they work. They are examples only.
The owners have not said their idea is like any of them.

## Do not assume
- Do not assume how a meal reaches the customer, or who carries it.
- Do not assume how pricing or payment works.
- Do not assume who the cooks are beyond people who cook at home.
