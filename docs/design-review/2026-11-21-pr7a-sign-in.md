# Design review: M1 PR 7a (home, sign-in, project list)

Branch `feature/client-sign-in`, demo build, checked in the browser at 390px
and 1280px in English and Turkish on 2026-11-21. Screens: the signed-out home
(where the demo now opens), the `/sign-in` page (a labelled sample in the
demo), the project list as Burak and as an owner, and the Sign out control.

Nothing overflows sideways at 390px; the Sign out control is 44 × 44px; the
EN | TR switch is always visible; Turkish labels fit; demo stand-ins are
labelled ("This is the demo: no email is sent", "Sample sign-in link").

## Problems, in order of how much they hurt the first impression

1. **The demo's main action is hidden.** On the home page the blue primary
   button is "Email me a sign-in link", which does nothing in the demo, and
   "Try the demo" is a secondary button in a second card, below the fold on
   a phone. A visitor on a phone may never find the way in. Smallest fix: in
   the demo build only, put the "Try the demo" card first and make its
   button primary; keep the email form below it, still labelled.
2. **The app name is cut off on phones once signed in.** The header shows
   "Idea Discovery Roo…" next to the language switch and the sign-out icon.
   A truncated product name looks unfinished. Smallest fix: let the name
   wrap to two lines, as it already does on the signed-out home, or show the
   mark only.
3. **Burak sees owner wording on his list.** As Burak the list says "Your
   projects — The ideas you are working on with your consultant", and with
   no projects he would see "You're not in any project yet. Your consultant
   can invite you." He is the consultant. Smallest fix: consultant-specific
   title, subtitle and empty state (English only, like his other screens),
   for example "Projects — Every idea you are helping with" and "No
   projects yet."
4. **Two lists for Burak.** On desktop the sidebar still offers "All rooms"
   (`/rooms`) while the main page is now the project list at `/`. Smallest
   fix: point the sidebar entry at the new list and drop the old route in
   the demo, or rename the entry so the two do not compete.
5. **Sign out is an unlabelled icon on phones.** The icon has an accessible
   name, but owners may not recognise it. Smallest fix: show the word
   ("Sign out" / "Çıkış yap") at 390px too if it fits next to EN | TR, or
   move it into the project list page as a quiet button.
6. **Sparse pages on desktop.** The home and sign-in pages are a single
   narrow card on a wide empty background. Acceptable for a sign-in page,
   but it reads more like a form than a product. Smallest fix (optional):
   the generic illustration from the idea page beside the card at 1280px.

## Three changes that would improve the look most
1. Make "Try the demo" the first and primary action in the demo build.
2. Stop truncating the app name in the signed-in phone header.
3. Give Burak his own wording on the project list, and one list rather
   than two.
