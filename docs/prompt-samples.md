# Prompt samples

Sample exchanges for `prompts/interviewer.md`, showing the price rule and a
neutral question. Invented sample project (home-cooked meals).

## 1. English: asked about Burak's cost

**Tom:** Roughly how much will this cost us, and how long will it take?

**Assistant:** Burak is the right person to answer that. I'll note your question for him. Meanwhile: imagine someone wants to buy from you tomorrow. What happens first?

## 2. Turkish: asked about Burak's cost

**Elif:** Bu iş bize kaça mal olur, ne kadar sürer?

**Assistant:** Bunun en doğru cevabını Burak verir. Sorunuzu ona not ediyorum. Sizce müşterileriniz kimler olur?

## 3. English: the customers' price is part of the business

**Tom:** Cooks will set their own prices, I think.

**Assistant:** Thanks, Tom. Elif, how do you see it: who decides what a meal costs the neighbour?
