# Pallet project — tentative room context

**Purpose:** Give the discovery AI enough background to start the conversation without making assumptions. **All items labelled “initial understanding” require owner confirmation.**

## People and process

- Three people are considering a UK pallet-transport business; one owner prefers English / is British (per current decisions log).
- One software consultant is trying to understand their **business concept**, not yet their detailed app requirements.
- They can respond at different times; assistant replies wait for the consultant's approval by default.
- The owners can use a printed bilingual feedback document as an alternative to the chat. Paper-answer ingestion is **not decided**.
- The earlier brief conversation on foot was **not** a first discovery meeting. Its Turkish voice transcription was unreliable in places.

## Current initial understanding — not confirmed

A preliminary picture is **transporting prepared pallets between warehouses or businesses**. The short conversation suggested (but did not definitively establish):

- Drivers would primarily transport goods rather than pack them.
- Loading/unloading might be done by the sender/receiver, not the driver.
- Waiting-time charges might be considered.
- An initial own van might be used before additional drivers.

**Avoid declaring any of these to be a settled owner requirement.** The very first question should invite them to describe the idea freely, rather than feeding these points back as truths.

## Open questions for later, not a first-message checklist

- What does the business actually do, and for whom?
- What happens in one ordinary job, start to finish?
- Who initiates a transport request and who decides how a driver takes it?
- Do the three owners imagine the same process or different variations?

Pricing, commission, payments, software features, logistics exceptions, liability and build scope may matter eventually. Do **not** start the conversation with them. They can be revisited once the basic story is established.

## First shared summary target

At most: a few owners' statements with attribution; a few early interpretations needing confirmation; up to three open questions. No features, pricing calculations or implementation architecture.
